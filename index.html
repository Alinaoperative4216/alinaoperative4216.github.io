🔍 What is socorin?

socorin is a free, open-source program that helps you capture anything on your computer screen. Whether you need to take a quick screenshot, mark up an image with arrows and text, or record a video of your screen, socorin does it all in one simple, fast app.

Think of it as your personal screen toolbox. It runs on Windows, macOS, and Linux, so it works no matter what computer you use. Unlike other tools that are heavy and slow, socorin is lightweight - it uses minimal memory and starts instantly.

🛠️ How to Use socorin

📷 Taking a Screenshot

When you open socorin, you'll see a small toolbar. Click the camera icon. Your screen will dim slightly. Click and drag with your mouse to select the area you want to capture, then release. Your screenshot appears instantly, ready to save or edit.

✍️ Annotating Your Screenshot

After taking a screenshot, a simple editing toolbar appears at the top. You can draw rectangles, circles, arrows, add text, or use the highlighter tool. Use the eraser to fix mistakes. When you're happy, click the Save button (the floppy disk icon).

🎬 Recording Your Screen

Click the video camera icon in the main toolbar. Select the area of your screen to record, then click the red Record button. A countdown will start (3-2-1), and then recording begins. When you're done, click the square Stop button in the floating control bar. Your video will be saved as an MP4 file.

💾 Saving Your Work

socorin saves screenshots as PNG or JPG images and recordings as MP4 video files. When you save, a window will appear letting you choose where to put the file and what to name it. Your files are ready to share, email, or upload right away.
